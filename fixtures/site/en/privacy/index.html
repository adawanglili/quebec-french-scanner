Privacy policy

We collect your name, address and email address when you place an order. We use them only to process the order and to contact you about it.

If you agree, we may send you an email when a new coffee arrives. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Our site uses cookies to keep your shopping cart. You can refuse them in your browser settings.

To access or correct your personal information, write to our privacy officer at vieprivee@northwind.example.
